Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains what Footing collects, how it’s used, and who it reaches. A lot of what Footing handles is health information: food logs, weight, GLP-1 medication and how you feel on it, and optional Apple Health or Health Connect data. So we’ve been specific rather than generic.

The short version

We don’t sell your data.Not to advertisers, not to anyone. It’s used to run Footing for you.
Pulse asks first.Nothing goes to our AI provider for Pulse until you agree, and you can turn Pulse off.
You decide what Pulse knows.Allergies, eating patterns and food likes are yours to add, change or clear.
Delete it all, any time.Profile → Delete Account removes your account and everything in it, immediately.

1. Information we collect

CategoryWhat
Account and profile
Email address. If you complete onboarding: name, date of birth, sex, height, activity level and weight goal.
Food, water and goals
Food and water entries (typed, searched, scanned, or described in your own words, typed or spoken), favorites, your calorie, macro and protein-floor goals, and food-access choices such as “rarely cook” or “eat out often”.
Body
Weight, body fat, lean mass, BMI, body measurements you track (waist, hips, chest, upper arm, thigh), and body goals.
Workouts
Workouts you log, and ones imported from Apple Health or Health Connect.
GLP-1 tracking
Only if you use it: medication, dose, injection site and time, doses you mark as skipped, shot-cycle check-ins (appetite, fullness, nausea, energy, digestion and an optional note), and whether tracking is active, paused or stopped.

Optional: allergies and an allergy note, eating patterns (for example vegetarian or halal), foods you love and foods you’d rather skip. Pulse can suggest adding something you mention in chat, but nothing is saved until you tap Save, and it never guesses an allergy.
Goals and experiments
Personal goals and their daily check-ins, and personal experiments with their measurements and results.
Pulse
Messages you send and the replies, including suggested foods, follow-ups and weekly recap cards. Your Strong Week check-in answers (for example “traveling” or “injured”) and whether you found an outlook useful. Your Pulse settings: on or off, shown on Today, and when you agreed to AI data sharing.
Voice input
Optional. Your speech is turned into text by your device’s speech service (Apple Speech Recognition on iPhone, or your Android speech service). Footing doesn’t record or store audio, only the resulting text.
Apple Health / Health Connect
Optional, and only the categories you approve: steps, distance, exercise, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, active energy, weight, body fat and lean mass. With write access, Footing can save the workouts, weight, body fat and lean mass you log back to Apple Health or Health Connect.
Feedback
Anything you send through Profile → Send Feedback.
Usage analytics
Anonymous product signals, like “a log was saved” or “a chat message was sent”. Never what you logged or said.

2. How we use it

  • To run Footing: tracking nutrition, showing your trends and goals, reminders you turn on, and Pulse.
  • To understand how Footing is used, in aggregate, so we know what to fix. We don’t read individual logs or conversations.
  • To answer feedback and support requests.

When Pulse is used

Pulse only runs after you agree. The first time you open Pulse, Footing shows what will be sent and asks. If you decline, or later turn Pulse off in Profile, nothing is sent to our AI provider for Pulse.

When Pulse answers you, it receives your message plus a snapshot of what it needs to be specific: your first name and units, today’s log and totals, the last 7 days, your goals, weight trend and workouts, connected Health data, what Pulse knows about you, and your GLP-1 schedule and today’s check-in. GLP-1 details are left out entirely while tracking is paused or stopped. Monday Recap also uses last week’s daily totals, and Strong Week uses your check-in answers.

Talking a meal into the log is separate from Pulse: it sends only the food description you typed or said.

3. Who we share it with

We don’t sell your data. It reaches only the providers that make Footing work, each limited to what it needs.

ProviderPurpose and what they receive
Supabase
Database, sign-in and server functions. Stores all the account and log data above.
Anthropic (Claude)
Powers Pulse and talk-to-log. Receives your message and the snapshot described in section 2, Strong Week check-in answers, and food descriptions for talk-to-log.
FatSecret
Food search and barcode lookup. Receives the food name or barcode you search for, not who you are.
TelemetryDeck
Anonymous usage analytics. Receives usage signals only: no log content, messages or personal details.
Apple
Sign in with Apple, HealthKit, notifications and speech recognition, under Apple’s own privacy terms.
Google / Android services
Google sign-in, Health Connect, notifications, barcode scanning and optional speech recognition on Android, under their own terms. Footing doesn’t send your Health Connect data to Google.

4. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. Profile → Delete Account permanently deletes your account and everything stored with it: logs, goals, body and weight history, GLP-1 records and check-ins, what Pulse knows about you, goals and experiments, chat history and feedback. It’s immediate and can’t be undone.

You can also clear just your Pulse chat history from Profile, or remove anything Pulse knows from Profile → What Pulse knows. Records you asked Footing to write into Apple Health or Health Connect stay there; manage them in that app.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our database provider. Access is scoped to your account by row-level security, so no other user can read it, and Pulse only ever sees your own data.

6. Age

Footing is for adults 18 and over (see the Terms). We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

7. Your choices and rights

Everything Footing stores is visible in the app, and you can correct or delete it there. There’s no export button yet: email us and we’ll send you a copy of your data. For anything else we haven’t made self-serve, get in touch.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we’ll update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@tryfooting.app, or see the Contact page.

Footing Coached, not scolded.

Footing is a personal nutrition and wellness tracker, not a medical device. It doesn’t diagnose, treat, or give dosing guidance. Always talk to your doctor about medication and health decisions.
