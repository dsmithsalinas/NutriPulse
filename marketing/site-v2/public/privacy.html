Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Footing collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. Footing is a nutrition and wellness tracking app; some of what it handles — food logs, weight, GLP-1 medication schedules, and optional Apple Health or Android Health Connect data — is health information, so we've tried to be specific rather than generic here.

1. Information we collect

CategoryWhat
Account Email address, and name/sex/date of birth/activity level if you complete onboarding.
Nutrition & activity logs Food and water entries (manual, searched, scanned, or described in your own words to Pulse — typed or spoken), daily macro/calorie goals, and body weight you log.
Voice input (optional) When you use voice input to speak a food log, your speech is transcribed by the speech-recognition service on your device (Apple Speech Recognition on iOS or your configured Android speech service). Footing doesn't record or store the audio — only the resulting text becomes a log. This requires the permissions requested by that service.
GLP-1 tracking Medication, dose, injection site and timing — only if you choose to use this feature.
Apple Health / Health Connect (optional) Depending on your device: steps, distance, exercise, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, active energy, weight, body-fat percentage, and lean body mass. Footing reads only the categories you approve, to show them to you, import selected records into your Footing account, and give Pulse context. When you approve write access, Footing can also save workouts, weight, body fat, and lean mass that you log in Footing back to Apple Health or Android Health Connect.
Pulse chat Messages you send to the in-app AI coach, and the responses it gives.
Feedback Anything you submit via the in-app “Send Feedback” form.
Usage analytics Anonymous product-usage signals (e.g. a log was saved, a chat message was sent) — never the content of what you logged or said.

2. How we use it

  • To run the core features: tracking your nutrition, showing your trends, and powering Pulse's coaching.
  • To give Pulse the context it needs to answer well — when you message Pulse, a snapshot of your relevant data (today's log, recent history, goals, weight trend, connected Health data, and GLP-1 schedule if applicable) is sent along with your message so it can respond specifically to you, not generically.
  • To understand product usage in aggregate, so we know what's working — never at the level of reading your individual logs or conversations.
  • To respond to feedback and support requests.

3. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. It's shared only with the service providers that make the app work, each limited to what they need to do their job:

ProviderPurposeWhat they receive
Supabase Database, authentication, file/edge-function hosting All account and log data described above — this is where it's stored.
Anthropic (Claude) Powers the Pulse AI coach and natural-language food logging Your message plus the data-context snapshot described above, and the food description you type or speak when using talk-to-log.
FatSecret Nutrition database for food search and barcode lookup The food name or barcode you search for. Not your account identity.
TelemetryDeck Anonymous product analytics Anonymous usage signals only — no log content, no messages, no personal identifiers.
Apple Sign in with Apple, HealthKit, push notifications, Speech Recognition (voice food logging) Standard to those Apple frameworks; governed by Apple's own privacy terms. Spoken food logs are transcribed by Apple's Speech Recognition.
Google / Android system services Google sign-in, Health Connect, notifications, camera barcode scanning, and optional Android speech recognition Standard to the Android services you choose to use and the permissions you grant; governed by the applicable provider's privacy terms. Footing does not send your Health Connect data to Google on its own.

4. Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete Account in the app. This is immediate and irreversible — it removes your logs, goals, weight history, GLP-1 records, chat history, and feedback stored with your Footing account. Records that you asked Footing to write into Apple Health or Health Connect remain in that connected health store and can be reviewed or deleted using its own controls.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest in our database provider. Access to your data is scoped to your account via row-level security — no other user can read it, and our AI coach only ever sees your own data when responding to you.

6. Children's privacy

Footing is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, or export your data through the app itself (it's designed to always show you exactly what's been logged), and delete your account and all data at any time as described above. If you have a request we haven't made self-service yet, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@tryfooting.app
