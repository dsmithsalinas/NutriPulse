Your data

Delete it yourself, any time Profile → Delete Account removes everything immediately. For a copy of your data, or any privacy question, email us.

Reporting a beta bug

What helps fix it fastest
  • What you were doing, and what you expected to happen.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can.
  • Your build number, from the Footing listing in the TestFlight app.
  • Never your password. We will never ask for it.

Not for emergencies

Footing and this inbox aren’t monitored around the clock.
