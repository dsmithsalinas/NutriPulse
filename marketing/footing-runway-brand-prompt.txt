FOOTING — BRAND STYLE PROMPT (paste into Runway as style/context)

Brand: Footing, a GLP-1 nutrition app. Its AI coach is named Pulse.

LOOK
The hero object is Footing's protein ring: one thick emissive arc, indigo to
violet, filling clockwise from twelve o'clock, with a white floor tick marking
a minimum at 76% around. It reads as light, not as a colored shape — soft
bloom, feathered edges, everything sharp (no depth of field). 3.5% film grain
and a moderate vignette over the whole frame. An emotional arc from near-black
to daylight: deep #07070F and #12122A at the dark end, cream #F6F5FC at the
light end.

COLOR
Indigo #6366F1 to violet #8B5CF6, always as emitted light or inner glow, never
as a flat fill or gradient blob. Warm amber #FF8A5B is the protein accent and
the only real color tension against the cool indigo. Green #34D399 only for
success. Ink #14163A for dark text.

TYPE
Inter / Inter Display, semibold, tight tracking (-0.035em), tabular numerals.
Instrument Serif italic, sparingly, for a single emotional pull-quote.
Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headlines. No Title Case.

VOICE (Pulse)
A cornerman, not a drill sergeant, cheerleader, or doctor. Attentive, precise,
warm-direct, steady, protective. Speaks in second person with contractions and
concrete numbers, always pairing a fact with the next move. Never shames anyone
for being on the medication, and never implies the medication does the work
alone. Frames everything as protecting progress, never correcting failure.
Essentially no exclamation marks, no hype, at most one earned emoji.
Sample register: "Coached, not scolded." / "One heavy day is data, not a
verdict." / "It's not about eating less. It's about eating enough."

NEVER
Neon glass or crypto/AI-startup gradients. Clinical white telehealth stock
photography or doctor imagery. Bright gamified quiz-app styling. Food
photography or plated-meal hero shots. Before/after bodies, scales, tape
measures, gym or bootcamp imagery. Syringes shown as the hero. Concrete,
slabs, or terrain — any literal reading of the word "footing" was prototyped
and cut. Words like journey, transform, unlock, crush it, cheat day,
guilt-free. Exclamation marks.
